The TALKTHERA Difference

Every member of our clinical faculty undergoes rigorous vetting, continuous education, and holds active national accreditation.

ASHA CCC-SLP Certified

100% of our clinical team holds the prestigious Certificate of Clinical Competence in Speech-Language Pathology from the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association.

Multidisciplinary Collaboration

Our specialists consult directly with ENTs, pediatricians, neurologists, and occupational therapists to craft holistic, patient-centered rehabilitation plans.
