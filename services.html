Decision Guide

Which Service Fits Your Needs?

Identify common communication signs and understand which therapy program is recommended for you or your loved one.

Speech Sounds

Sound Errors & Lisps

Noticeable trouble pronouncing letters like R, S, L, TH or substituting sounds (e.g. "wabbit" for "rabbit").

Recommended Solution: Articulation Therapy
Early Development

Late Talkers & Vocabulary

Fewer than 50 words by age 2, difficulty putting 2-word phrases together, or trouble understanding simple directions.

Recommended Solution: Language Delay Program
Adult Neuro

Stroke & Word Retrieval

Struggling to find words, speaking in fragmented phrases, or confusion understanding conversations following stroke or brain injury.

Recommended Solution: Adult Aphasia Rehabilitation
Vocal Care

Chronic Hoarseness & Strain

Breathy, strained voice, throat discomfort after teaching or speaking, vocal nodule diagnosis, or voice pitch fatigue.

Recommended Solution: Voice Therapy & Hygiene
ASD & Assistive

Social Connection & AAC

Challenges with eye contact, social pragmatics, or child requires visual schedules, PECS, or digital speech devices.

Recommended Solution: Autism & AAC Support
Medical Care

Choking & Swallowing Pain

Coughing during meals, taking prolonged time to chew, feeling food stuck, or recovering from head/neck procedures.

Recommended Solution: Swallowing Therapy (Dysphagia)
