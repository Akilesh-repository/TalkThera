The Basics

General Questions

Information about starting therapy, our facility, and what to expect during your visits.

While you do not always need a doctor's referral to receive an initial evaluation or start private-pay therapy, most insurance companies require a referral from your primary care physician or a specialist (like an ENT or Neurologist) in order to cover the costs. We recommend checking with your specific insurance provider.

The initial evaluation typically lasts between 60 to 90 minutes. For children, it involves play-based interactions and standardized tests. For adults, it includes conversational tasks and specific diagnostic tests. We will review medical history, assess current skills, discuss your goals, and formulate a customized treatment plan.

A standard therapy session usually lasts for 45 minutes. This includes 35-40 minutes of direct therapeutic intervention and 5-10 minutes for consulting with parents/caregivers, reviewing progress, and providing home exercise materials. We also offer 30-minute and 60-minute sessions depending on the patient's stamina and specific needs.

Frequency varies greatly depending on the severity of the diagnosis and the specific goals outlined in the care plan. Most of our clients attend therapy once or twice a week. Your speech-language pathologist will recommend the optimal frequency after the initial evaluation.

Yes, absolutely! We strongly encourage parent involvement, especially for early intervention and pediatric language delays. Parents observing and participating in sessions is one of the most effective ways to ensure that therapeutic strategies are carried over into the home environment.

Yes, we offer secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth services for clients who prefer to receive therapy from home. Telepractice is highly effective for many conditions, including adult aphasia, voice therapy, and articulation therapy for older children and adults. However, it may not be suitable for very young children with severe attention deficits.

Details & Administration

Clinical & Billing

Specific information regarding our therapeutic approach, insurance coverage, and payment options.

We are in-network with many major insurance providers, including Medicare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, and Aetna. Because coverage for speech therapy can vary wildly between specific plans, we recommend calling our office with your insurance information so our billing team can verify your benefits prior to your first visit.

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), debit cards, HSA/FSA cards, and personal checks. For private pay clients or those covering deductibles, payment is expected at the time of service.

We require a 24-hour notice for cancellations to avoid a cancellation fee. Consistent attendance is crucial for therapeutic progress. Frequent cancellations or no-shows may result in the loss of your recurring appointment slot.

Progress varies significantly depending on the individual, their diagnosis, consistency of attendance, and the amount of home practice completed. Some clients see notable changes within a few weeks, while chronic or severe conditions may require months or years of sustained intervention. We continuously monitor and report on progress.

Yes. Our clinicians are specially trained in the evaluation and treatment of dysphagia (swallowing disorders) in both pediatric and adult populations. This includes clinical bedside swallowing evaluations, diet modification recommendations, and oral-motor strengthening exercises.

Absolutely. We believe a collaborative approach yields the best results. With your signed consent, we are happy to communicate and coordinate care with your child's teachers, school-based speech therapists, occupational therapists, physical therapists, or primary care physicians.
